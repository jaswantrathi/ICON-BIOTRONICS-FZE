ICON BIOTRONICS respects the privacy of website visitors, customers, healthcare facilities, technical partners, and other individuals who communicate with us. We aim to handle personal information responsibly and only for legitimate business and service-related purposes.

Limited Collection We collect information needed to answer enquiries and provide technical services.
Appropriate Protection Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect submitted information.
No Unauthorised Sale We do not sell personal information submitted through this website.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the ICON BIOTRONICS website, contact forms, email communications, telephone calls, WhatsApp messages, service enquiries, quotations, inspections, maintenance requests, and related business interactions.

By using this website or voluntarily submitting information, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Information you provide

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your full name.
  • Your company, hospital, clinic, laboratory, or facility.
  • Your email address and telephone number.
  • Your location or service address.
  • The service you require.
  • Medical equipment type, brand, model, and serial number.
  • Equipment faults, error codes, and maintenance history.
  • Photographs, videos, reports, or technical documents.
  • Quotation, contract, invoicing, or payment-related details.
  • Other information included in your message.

Technical website information

Our server or service providers may automatically record limited technical information, such as:

  • Internet Protocol address.
  • Browser and device type.
  • Operating system.
  • Date and time of access.
  • Referring website or page.
  • Pages viewed and general website activity.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and service requests.
  • Assess biomedical engineering requirements.
  • Prepare quotations, proposals, and maintenance plans.
  • Coordinate inspections, repairs, installations, and visits.
  • Identify equipment and diagnose reported technical issues.
  • Communicate with customers and authorised representatives.
  • Maintain service, maintenance, and calibration records.
  • Process contracts, invoices, and business documentation.
  • Improve website performance and customer communication.
  • Prevent spam, misuse, fraud, and security threats.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Depending on the context, we may process information based on:

  • Your consent.
  • Your request for information, a quotation, or a service.
  • The performance or preparation of a contract.
  • Our legitimate business interests.
  • Compliance with applicable laws and regulatory duties.
  • The establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims.

5. Information Sharing

ICON BIOTRONICS does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary with:

  • Authorised employees and biomedical engineers.
  • Approved service partners or subcontractors.
  • Equipment manufacturers, distributors, or spare-parts suppliers.
  • Hosting, email, security, and information technology providers.
  • Professional advisers, auditors, insurers, or legal representatives.
  • Government, regulatory, or law-enforcement authorities where required.

Third parties are expected to use information only for the purpose for which it was provided and to apply appropriate confidentiality and security measures.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including service delivery, maintenance records, customer support, legal compliance, accounting, warranty, dispute resolution, and legitimate business needs.

Retention periods may vary according to the type of information, the equipment involved, contractual requirements, and applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable organisational, administrative, and technical measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet transmission, email system, website, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should avoid sending highly sensitive information through unsecured communication channels.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use essential cookies or similar technologies required for security, form functionality, user preferences, performance monitoring, or website operation.

If analytics or non-essential cookies are introduced, the website should provide an appropriate consent notice where required.

9. Third-Party Services

The website may use or link to third-party services, including:

  • Google Maps.
  • WhatsApp.
  • Google Fonts.
  • Font Awesome.
  • Unsplash-hosted images.
  • Website hosting and email providers.

These third parties may process technical information according to their own privacy policies. ICON BIOTRONICS is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites or services.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and relevant exceptions, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion where retention is no longer necessary.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request information about how your data is used.
  • Submit a complaint to an appropriate authority.

We may need to verify your identity before responding to a privacy request.

11. Children's Privacy

This website and our biomedical engineering services are intended for businesses, healthcare organisations, and adult professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our website, services, legal obligations, or data-handling practices. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective or last-updated date.
