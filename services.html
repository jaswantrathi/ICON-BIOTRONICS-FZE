FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Biomedical Engineering Service Questions

Which areas of the UAE do you cover?

ICON BIOTRONICS provides biomedical engineering support across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah, subject to engineer availability and service requirements.

Do you provide emergency medical equipment repair?

Yes. Urgent breakdown support is available for critical medical equipment. Customers can call or contact our team through WhatsApp for immediate assistance.

Can you maintain equipment from multiple manufacturers?

Yes. Our team supports a wide range of brands and equipment types. Service capability is confirmed after reviewing the model, fault description and technical requirements.

Do you offer annual maintenance contracts?

Yes. Preventive, comprehensive and multi-site maintenance plans can be tailored to the customer's equipment inventory and operational priorities.

Do you provide calibration certificates?

Calibration and test documentation can be provided based on the equipment category, measurement requirements and agreed service scope.

Need Medical Equipment Repair or Maintenance?

Send us the equipment brand, model, fault description and location. Our technical team will review your request and recommend the next step.
