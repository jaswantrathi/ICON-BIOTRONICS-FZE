WHAT TO DO FIRST

Prepare the Information Our Engineer Needs

Clear information helps us assess the urgency and determine the correct technical response.

1

Remove the Equipment From Use

Label the equipment as faulty and prevent further clinical use when safety may be affected.

2

Record the Fault

Note error codes, alarm messages, unusual sounds, visible damage or recent events.

3

Photograph the Equipment Label

Capture the brand, model, serial number and equipment identification plate.

4

Share the Facility Location

Include the emirate, facility name, department and contact person.

5

State the Operational Impact

Explain whether the equipment is fully unavailable, intermittent or affecting patient services.

Important safety notice

Do not open, modify or attempt internal repairs unless you are qualified and authorised to service the equipment.

EMERGENCY SUPPORT FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide 24-hour emergency service?

Emergency requests may be accepted outside normal working hours, but response is subject to engineer availability, location, equipment type and access arrangements.

How quickly can an engineer attend?

Attendance depends on the current workload, facility location, traffic, equipment category, access approval and technical requirements. The team will provide the available next step after reviewing the request.

What information should I send on WhatsApp?

Send the equipment category, brand, model, serial number, error code, photos, fault description, facility name, department and contact person.

Can you repair all medical equipment brands?

Support depends on equipment type, technical documentation, software access, spare-part availability and manufacturer restrictions.

Will you provide a service report?

Service documentation may include reported fault, findings, work performed, test results, parts used and recommendations, depending on the service scope.
