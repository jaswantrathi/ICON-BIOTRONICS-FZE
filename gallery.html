Are all gallery images from completed service projects?

The gallery is intended to showcase representative biomedical engineering work, equipment categories and service capabilities. Images should only be published when suitable customer permissions and confidentiality requirements have been addressed.

Service capability depends on the equipment manufacturer, model, technical condition and parts availability. Send the brand, model and fault description to our team for confirmation.

Project photographs and technical findings can be included in service documentation when appropriate and permitted by the healthcare facility.

Yes. ICON BIOTRONICS can coordinate maintenance and repair support across multiple equipment categories and facility locations.
