WHO WE ARE

A Trusted Technical Partner for Healthcare Facilities

ICON BIOTRONICS is a biomedical engineering services company focused on helping hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centres, laboratories and dental facilities maintain safe, dependable and high-performing medical equipment.

Our engineers provide practical technical support throughout the equipment lifecycle, from installation and commissioning to preventive maintenance, breakdown repair, calibration, validation and asset management.

We combine engineering expertise, responsive service and detailed documentation to help healthcare organisations reduce equipment downtime, improve reliability and support patient safety.

QUALITY APPROACH

A Structured Process for Reliable Service Delivery

Every service assignment follows a clear workflow designed to improve accuracy, traceability and customer confidence.

  1. 01

    Initial Assessment

    We review the equipment condition, reported issue, service history and operational environment.

  2. 02

    Technical Diagnosis

    Engineers perform visual inspection, functional tests and fault isolation using suitable test instruments.

  3. 03

    Repair or Maintenance

    Corrective or preventive work is completed using appropriate procedures and replacement parts.

  4. 04

    Testing and Documentation

    Equipment is verified before handover and the completed work is recorded in a service report.
