FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Certification and Qualification Questions

Can I request copies of your technical certificates?

Yes. Relevant qualification or training documents may be shared with customers, procurement teams or healthcare organisations when appropriate and subject to privacy requirements.

Are all certificates applicable to every equipment type?

No. Certificate scope varies by training provider, equipment category, manufacturer and technical subject. Applicability should be confirmed for each service requirement.

Do certificates guarantee manufacturer-authorised service?

Not necessarily. Manufacturer-authorised status should only be claimed when supported by current, verifiable authorisation from that manufacturer.

Can you provide calibration and service documentation?

Yes. Service reports, test records and calibration-related documentation may be provided according to the agreed service scope and equipment category.

How can I verify certificate validity?

Contact ICON BIOTRONICS with the certificate title or number. Where appropriate, validity can also be checked directly with the issuing organisation.
