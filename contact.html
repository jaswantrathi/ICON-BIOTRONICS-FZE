Frequently Asked Questions

Before You Submit an Enquiry

What information should I include in my request?

Include the equipment type, manufacturer, model, facility location, reported issue, urgency, and any available error codes or photographs.

Do you provide service outside Dubai?

Yes. ICON BIOTRONICS supports healthcare facilities and medical equipment organisations across the United Arab Emirates, subject to scheduling and service scope.

Can I request an annual maintenance contract?

Yes. Provide your equipment list and facility requirements so the team can review the scope and prepare a suitable maintenance proposal.

Do you handle urgent breakdowns?

Urgent requests can be discussed by telephone or WhatsApp. Response availability depends on the equipment category, location, spare-parts requirements, and engineer scheduling.

Can I send equipment photos through WhatsApp?

Yes. Photographs of the equipment, model label, error display, and damaged components can help the technical team understand the issue before attendance.
